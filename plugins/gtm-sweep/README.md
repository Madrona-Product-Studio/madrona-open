# gtm-sweep — take an app to market as a measured sweep, not a vibe

Most side projects and studio apps never get *marketed* — they get launched
once, quietly, with no measurement floor, and the maker concludes "nobody
wanted it." This skill runs go-to-market as a repeatable sweep with a read
loop, so every launch teaches you something you can use on the next one.

## The phases

- **Phase 0 — Audit.** What does this app claim, to whom, and can we measure
  anything yet? No channel work until positioning and analytics exist.
- **Phase 1 — Positioning + measurement floor.** A one-liner and wedge that
  survive a stranger's first 10 seconds, plus the minimum analytics to know
  if anything works.
- **Phase 2 — Discoverability.** SEO expansion: meta, OG, sitemap,
  structured data, and content routes crawlers can actually read.
- **Phase 3 — Channel launch.** Pick the channels that fit the app, sequence
  them, ship with the entry point clean.
- **Phase 4 — Read + iterate.** Return to the numbers on a rhythm; double
  down or kill based on what they say.

## The compounding part

Every real play run through the skill gets banked in a **Learned plays**
section — what was tried, what it cost, what it returned — so the playbook
gets smarter with each app instead of starting from zero.

Pairs with `investor-ready` (outsider clarity before traffic arrives) and
`og-image` (the first impression in every share).
