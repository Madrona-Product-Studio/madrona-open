_This file holds the durable judgment about what matters and why. The routines read it to know how to rank, what to push, and what to protect. Edit it when your priorities genuinely change — not every day. `work.md` holds the live tasks; this holds the reasoning behind them._

## Decision heuristics

_How to break ties and rank. A few plain rules. Examples:_
- **Urgency × impact × momentum.** Prefer work that is time-sensitive, high-impact, and already moving.
- **Protect the floor.** The protected area is never crowded out, no matter how hot everything else is.
- **When a task contradicts strategy, surface the conflict** rather than silently following the task list.

## The ⭐ convention (how priorities work)

- A task becomes a top priority by getting a `⭐` **on the task itself, in its own section** of `work.md`.
- There is **no separate "top priorities" list.** The ranked blend is *generated* from the ⭐ tasks by `/prioritize`, not hand-maintained.
- Only you decide what earns a ⭐. Routines propose stars; they don't add them on their own judgment.

## The operating rhythm

_The cadence that keeps the system alive. Adjust to your life._
- **Daily** — `/daily-brief`: ingest what changed, re-rank, produce one decision surface for the day.
- **Weekly** — `/weekly-sync`: the conductor. What moved, what's owed, what's slipping; refresh the Owed and Track Status blocks.
- **Anytime** — `/capture`: say something once and it files itself into the right section.
